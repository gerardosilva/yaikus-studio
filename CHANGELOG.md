# Changelog

## 0.1.0
First public release (free). Native macOS app.
- RSS feeds and links → news list → one video per story.
- Bring your own AI inside the app (Claude, OpenAI-compatible APIs) or from outside over **MCP** (Claude Code, OpenClaw, Hermes…).
- Editable rules and local validators; TikTok, Reels, Shorts (vertical) and YouTube (horizontal) formats.
- Bring your own voice (Mac voice, OpenAI-compatible API or ElevenLabs), karaoke captions, backgrounds: the story's image, your own file or Pexels footage.
- Native AVFoundation render: no Python, no ffmpeg, no extra installs.
- English and Spanish interface, keys in the Keychain, new-version notice.
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
