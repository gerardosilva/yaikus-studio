## Yaikus Studio 0.1.0 — first public release 🎬

Turn today's news into short videos with **your own AI**. A free, open-source Mac app for creators who are just getting started — and for anyone who wants their AI agent to do the heavy lifting.

**What's inside**
- Add RSS feeds or article links → pick a story → get a validated script → preview → export an MP4.
- **Your rules:** editable playbook (tone, word limits, banned phrases, language, outro). The app checks every script against it.
- **TikTok, Reels, Shorts** (vertical 9:16) and **YouTube** (horizontal 16:9).
- **Bring your own AI:** Claude or any OpenAI-compatible API — or drive the app from your own agent through the built-in **MCP server** (Claude Code, OpenClaw, Hermes, Grok bots…).
- **Bring your own voice:** built-in Mac voice (free, offline), OpenAI-compatible TTS, or ElevenLabs.
- Backgrounds: story image, your own image/video, or royalty-free footage from Pexels.
- Native macOS app (14+), English and Spanish, no accounts, no telemetry, API keys in the Keychain.

**Install:** download the `.dmg` below, drag the app to Applications. This early release isn't notarized yet, so on first launch go to *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*. Verify the download with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**Known limits (v0.1):** not notarized; the Intel build hasn't been tested on real hardware; Pexels and ElevenLabs haven't been run against live accounts.

**Next:** notarized builds.
